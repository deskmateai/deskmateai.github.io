DeskMate AI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

To sell and support the Service we keep: the business name, contact email, license key, subscription status, the Google account email where the Service is installed, the date the Terms were accepted, and the Service version. Payments are processed by Stripe; we never see full card numbers.

Customer data stays in the customer's account

The Service runs inside the Customer's own Google account. Emails, calendar events, leads and logs are stored in the Customer's Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Sheets — not on our servers. We do not have access to them unless the Customer gives it to us for support.

AI processing

To write replies, the text of incoming emails and the Customer's business information are sent to Google's Gemini API using the Customer's own API key. Google processes this data under its own terms. On Google's free tier, Google may use submitted content to improve its products; customers who prefer otherwise can enable billing on their key (paid tier). The Service stores only email metadata (date, sender, subject, category) in the Customer's Leads and Log tabs, not full email bodies.

Demo page

Text typed into the public demo is sent to Gemini to generate the example reply and is not stored by us. Please do not enter real personal information in the demo.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share data only with providers needed to run the Service (Stripe for payments, Google for hosting and AI).

Your choices

Customers can delete the Control Panel and the script at any time, which removes the Service and its data from their account. To ask about or delete the data we keep for billing, contact DeskMateAi@proton.me.
